# v1.0.0

**An AI tutor that is not allowed to explain first.**

一个**不允许先讲解**的 AI 导师。它逼你先输出，然后测你，然后把复习排进日程。

---

## Why

Ask an AI to teach you something and you get a fluent explanation. You finish it feeling
like you understood. A week later you can't reproduce any of it.

让 AI 教你一个东西，你会得到一份读起来很顺的讲解。读完你觉得懂了。一周后一个字都复现不出来。

This is measurable, not just a hunch. Bastani et al. (2025, *PNAS*), ~1,000 high-school
students, two AI tutors:

| Group | While using the AI | **After the AI was removed** |
|---|---|---|
| Raw ChatGPT-style assistant | **+48%** | **17% worse than students who never had access** |
| Tutor with learning guardrails | **+127%** | no negative effect |

**The failure mode isn't "the AI explained badly". It's "the AI explained".**

---

## What's in it

- **8 hard rules** — never answer first; one small chunk at a time; every session ends
  with a test; a wrong answer triggers "how did you get there?" before any correction;
  only use vocabulary and analogies the learner has confirmed
- **9-step session loop** — load state → review due cards → pretest → teach →
  self-explain → restate → practice → mastery gate → record
- **Prep mode** — compiles source material (book, slides, transcript) into a lesson pack
  with 11 fields per atomic point, three-way labelling
  (`[verbatim]` / `[added by me]` / `[unverified]`), and **gaps written as gaps** rather
  than invented
- **Learner profile** — a global, cross-subject file: knowledge boundaries (tested vs
  self-reported), vocabulary that may be used, analogy library, calibration record
- **Spaced repetition** — `scripts/due.py` selects due cards; expanding intervals, 1–180 days
- **Full evidence base** — `references/methods.md` maps every rule to its source

## Install

```bash
cp -r learn ~/.pi/skills/     # user-level
# or
cp -r learn .pi/skills/       # project-level
```

Then `/reload` in an active session, or restart pi.

**Requirements:** pi · Python 3 (stdlib only, for `due.py`) · no network, no API keys.

## Use

```
/skill:learn teach me Fourier transforms
/skill:learn I don't understand the borrow checker
/skill:learn review software engineering
/skill:learn continue
```

## Grounding

Rosenshine 2012 · Dunlosky et al. 2013 · Hattie & Timperley 2007 · Sweller et al. 2019 ·
Bloom 1984 · Wilson et al. 2019 · Pan & Sana 2021 · Brunmair & Richter 2019 ·
Bastani et al. 2025 (*PNAS*) · Kestin et al. 2025 (*Scientific Reports*) ·
Wang et al. 2024 (Tutor CoPilot).

Deliberately **not** implemented: learning styles (debunked), growth mindset as a general
lever (downgraded), rereading/highlighting (low utility).

## Honest limitations

- **Long-term retention is unmeasured.** Every "mastered" verdict so far is a same-day
  measurement. The spaced-repetition schedule exists precisely because same-day
  performance is not evidence.
- **Prep mode has only been tested on structured books**, never on raw transcripts — the
  highest-risk source class it was designed for.
- **n = 1.** The mechanisms come from published research; this skill's implementation of
  them is not itself validated.
